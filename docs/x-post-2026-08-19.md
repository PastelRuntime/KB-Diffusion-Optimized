## The post (ready to paste)

Ran my first real ML experiments today. One evening, three full iterations, all on a free Kaggle GPU. Still buzzing honestly.

Started from @bijanbowen's KB-Diffusion repo. Tiny masked diffusion model that "denoises" a keyboard. Buried in the train file is a challenge: swap the 4 keyboard layouts for real words. So I did.

Iteration 1: trained it on 8,000 five-letter words. The big lesson jumped out immediately. Same exact weights, four ways of sampling:

- all 5 letters at once: 2% real words
- one letter at a time, re-asking after each: 68%
- greedy (always take the best guess): 100% valid but the SAME word 64 times in a row
- and it made words it never saw in training

The model never changed. The procedure did everything. The sampler is half the model.

Iteration 2: OK so if sampling matters that much, what else is hiding in there? Turns out temperature alone took that 68% to 95.5%. Same weights, same compute, one number changed. Also retrained a deeper version and hit 98.4%. But here's the thing: the old model interrogated well beat the new model interrogated badly. Decoding > parameters. Twice.

Iteration 3: the comeback story. I tested a "revision" sampler that can un-commit letters it regrets. At 5 letters it LOST, cost 4x more compute for worse output. At 10 letters it WON, 74% vs 63%. Longer sequences = early mistakes poison more of the word = being able to go back and fix them finally pays. That's the thing diffusion models can do that autoregressive models structurally can't, and I have a two data point curve showing where it kicks in.

Also my greedy model's favorite word at 10 letters is "repedating" which is not a word. 64 out of 64 times. I kind of love it.

Everything's public: code + writeups at https://github.com/PastelRuntime/KB-Diffusion-Optimized, weights on Hugging Face at https://huggingface.co/PastelRuntime/KB-Diffusion-ModelB, all reproducible on a free Kaggle account. First HF repo of my life. If you're sitting on three years of watching AI content and wondering if you can actually do the thing: you can, and the free tier is enough.

Not 100% sure why temperature 0.5 is the sweet spot at 5 letters but not at 10. Still chewing on that one.
